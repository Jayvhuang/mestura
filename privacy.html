Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-16. This policy is provided in English and 中文.

English

What we collect. Mestura's calculators (unit converter, recipe scaler, oven converter, substitutions, nutrition estimator, liquid measures, cooking-time, and serving-size tools) run entirely inside your web browser. The numbers and ingredients you type are processed on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

No accounts. There is no login, and we do not create user profiles for the tools.

Cookies & analytics. The tools themselves set no tracking cookies. This site may load privacy-friendly, aggregated analytics and, if you have consented where required, advertising cookies from Google AdSense. In the EEA/UK/CH, non-essential cookies are shown only after you grant consent via our cookie banner.

Affiliate links. Some product links are Amazon Associates links. If you click and buy, Amazon may set its own cookies under its policy; we earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Your rights. Because we do not store your inputs, there is nothing for us to delete. For questions, contact the site operator through the homepage.
