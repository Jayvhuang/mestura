FAQ

Are the nutrition numbers exact?

They are commonly published per-100g estimates for home planning. They are not a substitute for food labels or professional dietary advice.

营养数值是精确的吗？ — 它们是家庭规划常用的每 100 克公开估算值，不能替代食品标签或专业饮食建议。

How do I enter an ingredient in cups?

Choose the "cups" unit; the estimator converts to grams using that ingredient’s density before applying per-100g values.

如何以杯为单位输入食材？ — 选择“杯”单位，估算器会先按该食材密度转为克，再套用每 100 克数值。
