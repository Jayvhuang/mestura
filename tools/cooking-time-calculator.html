FAQ

How long do I roast a chicken per kg?

A whole chicken is roughly 55 minutes per kg at a standard oven temperature. Reduce time by about 20% in a convection oven.

每公斤鸡肉要烤多久？ — 整只鸡在标准烤箱温度下约每公斤 55 分钟。对流烤箱时间缩短约 20%。

How do I adjust time for a bigger roast?

Cooking time scales with weight to the power of 2/3, not linearly. New time ≈ original × (new weight ÷ original weight)^(2/3).

更大的烤肉时间如何调整？ — 烹饪时间随重量呈 2/3 次方变化，并非线性。新时间 ≈ 原时间 ×（新重量 ÷ 原重量）^(2/3)。
