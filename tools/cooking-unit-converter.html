FAQ

How do I convert cups to grams?

Pick the ingredient, enter the cups (or tbsp/tsp), and the converter multiplies by that ingredient’s weight per cup. All-purpose flour is about 125 g per cup.

杯怎么换算成克？ — 选择食材并输入杯数（或汤匙/茶匙），换算器会乘以该食材每杯的重量。中筋面粉每杯约 125 克。

Why are cups not the same as grams?

A cup is a volume measure; grams are weight. Flour, sugar and butter have different densities, so one cup weighs different amounts for each.

为什么杯和克不相等？ — 杯是容积单位，克是重量单位。面粉、糖和黄油密度不同，因此同样一杯的重量各不相同。
