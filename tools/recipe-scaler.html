FAQ

How do I halve a recipe?

Set original servings to the recipe’s amount and target servings to half of it. Each line is multiplied by the ratio.

如何把食谱减半？ — 把“原份数”设为食谱原本份数，“目标份数”设为一半，每行都会乘以这个比例。

What format should ingredients be in?

Use a leading number and a unit in words, then the ingredient, e.g. "2 cups flour". Lines without a number are left unchanged.

食材应该用什么格式？ — 用开头的数字加文字单位再写食材，例如“2 杯 面粉”。没有开头数字的行保持不变。
