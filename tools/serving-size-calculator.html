FAQ

How much rice per person should I buy?

Plan about 75 g of uncooked rice per person. The calculator totals it across your guest count and lets you adjust any item.

每人应购买多少米？ — 每人约准备 75 克生米。计算器会按人数汇总，并允许你调整任意食材。

Can I change the per-person amounts?

Yes, every per-person value is an editable field, so you can match your own recipes and preferences.

我可以修改每人用量吗？ — 可以，每个“每人用量”都是可编辑字段，可匹配你自己的食谱与偏好。
