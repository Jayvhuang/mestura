FAQ

What can I use instead of buttermilk?

Stir 1 tablespoon of lemon juice or white vinegar into 1 cup of milk and let it sit 5 minutes. Plain yogurt thinned with milk also works.

酪乳可以用什么替代？ — 在 1 杯牛奶中加入 1 汤匙柠檬汁或白醋，静置 5 分钟。用少许牛奶稀释的原味酸奶也可。

Can I replace an egg in baking?

A vegan binder is 1 tablespoon ground flaxseed plus 3 tablespoons water. Unsweetened applesauce adds moisture instead.

烘焙中可以用什么替代鸡蛋？ — 素食粘合剂可用 1 汤匙亚麻籽粉加 3 汤匙水。无糖苹果泥则提供保湿作用。
