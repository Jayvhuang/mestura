FAQ

How many ml are in a shot?

A standard shot is 1.5 fl oz, or about 44.4 ml.

一 shot 是多少毫升？ — 标准一 shot 为 1.5 液量盎司，约 44.4 毫升。

How do I convert fl oz to ml?

Multiply fluid ounces by 29.57 to get milliliters. For example, 8 fl oz is about 237 ml.

液量盎司如何换算成毫升？ — 液量盎司乘以 29.57 即为毫升。例如 8 液量盎司约等于 237 毫升。
