FAQ

What is 350°F in Celsius?

350°F is about 177°C, which is Gas Mark 4. A convection oven would use roughly 157°C.

350°F 是多少摄氏度？ — 350°F 约等于 177°C，即 Gas Mark 4。对流烤箱大约用 157°C。

How much do I lower the temperature for a fan oven?

Lower the temperature by about 20°C (25°F) and shorten the time by roughly 20%.

风炉烤箱温度要降低多少？ — 温度降低约 20°C（25°F），时间缩短约 20%。
